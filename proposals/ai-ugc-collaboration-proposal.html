贵司是中国领先的 AI UGC 培训机构，市场占有率约 60%，拥有成熟的「从 0 到变现」课程体系，并已手握大量广告 / 内容需求。贵司正进军马来西亚，目标人群为马来西亚华人。

合作逻辑很简单——「宣传换资源，只走分成」：J&Y 用自有渠道 + 素人裂变持续帮贵司宣传；作为回报，贵司提供免费试课名额、把正课开放给 J&Y 团队、后期让我们当导师。双方唯一的钱是正课分成，不另收服务费。

You are a leading AI UGC training company in China (~60% market share) with a proven "0 → monetization" curriculum and a large pipeline of advertiser/content demand. You are entering Malaysia, targeting the Malaysian-Chinese audience.

J & Y Agency is a Penang-based AI-driven KOC marketing agency (XHS, content, digital IP) with a proprietary KOC Builder AI system, a local Chinese audience, marketing channels, and a base of merchant clients who need content.
