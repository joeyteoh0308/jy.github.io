## 6. 商业模式（只走分成 + 宣传换资源）

我们刻意把模式做得**简单、对贵司友好**：

**① 唯一的钱 = 正课分成。**
- **引流课（如 RM19.9）：** 本就微利，**我们不分成**（或仅按成功招生人头给少量奖励，可谈）。
- **正课：** 才是真正赚钱处，J&Y 拿**合理一份、贵司为大头**——**具体比例待双方协商**（本提案不写死）。
- **不另收营销服务费**：我们的宣传就是换取分成与资源的投入，避免重复收费。

**② 其余用「宣传换资源」（非现金）——这也是 J&Y 的诉求：**
- ✅ 贵司提供**一定数量的免费试课名额**（给素人做裂变宣传，等于免费获客）；
- ✅ **正课免费开放**给 J&Y 创办人与团队（培养本地导师、统一交付标准）；
- ✅ 后期 J&Y 担任**认证导师**，同样**以分成形式**（不收死工资）。

**③ J&Y 自有的下游收入（与分成无关）：**
- 把培养出的 KOC 输送给 J&Y 商家客户 + 数字 IP 咨询所得（这部分是 J&Y 凭自己客户赚的）。

> 其他可选模式（仅供参考，不建议先用）：加盟 / 授权费、合资 JV。
> 我们建议**先用「分成 + 宣传换资源」做试点**，跑出成绩再谈升级。

## 6. Commercial Model (Revenue Share Only + Promotion-for-Access)

We deliberately keep it **simple and partner-friendly**:

**① The only money = full-course revenue share.**
- **Trial course (e.g. RM19.9):** thin-margin by nature — **we take no share** (or only a small bonus
  per successful enrolment, negotiable).
- **Full course:** the real revenue — J&Y takes a **reasonable share, Partner keeps the larger part**;
  the **exact % is to be negotiated** (not fixed in this proposal).
- **No separate marketing service fee** — our promotion *is* our contribution in exchange for the
  share and the access; we avoid double-charging.

**② Everything else is "promotion-for-access" (non-cash) — also J&Y's asks:**
- ✅ You provide a **number of free trial seats** (for grassroots creators to drive viral marketing —
  effectively free customer acquisition for you);
- ✅ **Free full-course access** for the J&Y founder & team (to build local trainers & consistent delivery);
- ✅ Later, J&Y serves as a **certified trainer**, also on a **revenue-share basis** (no flat salary).

**③ J&Y's own downstream income (separate from the share):**
- Supplying trained KOCs to J&Y merchant clients + digital-IP consulting (this is J&Y's own client revenue).

> Optional alternatives (for reference, not recommended first): license/franchise fee, JV.
> We recommend **starting with revenue share + promotion-for-access as a pilot**, then upgrading once proven.
