Sales Partner Program & Commission Agreement

Prepared by J & Y Marketing Agency, Georgetown, Penang  |  A partnership to grow together

1. The Partnership at a Glance

We're inviting you to become a Sales Partner for J & Y Agency. Your role is simple and plays to your strengths:

  • Find potential clients in your own way — your network, social media, outreach, referrals.
  • Qualify them — make sure they genuinely need our marketing / KOC / SEO services.
  • Book the appointment — set up the meeting and hand it over.
  • We close. Joey handles the pitch, the contract, and the delivery.

You focus on what you do best — opening doors. We focus on closing and delivering great work. And here's the best part: you keep earning every single month for as long as that client stays with us. Bring good clients who stay, and you build a growing monthly income.

2. How You Earn

Your rewards come in four layers — designed so you're paid instantly when you close, and continuously every month after.

Layer 1 — Your monthly income

20% of the client's monthly fee — every month they stay

For as long as the client is active and paying, you receive 20% of their total monthly fee. This never stops while they stay — it's your recurring, passive income.

Because it's a percentage of the total fee, the bigger the deal, the more you earn. A client on a RM350 basic plan pays you RM70/month. The same client who also takes SEO (RM250) pays a RM600 total — so you earn RM120/month instead. Add-ons reward you automatically, forever.

Layer 2 — Instant close bonus

A one-time cash bonus every time you close a new client

On top of your recurring income, you earn an upfront bonus for each newly signed, paid client. The more you close in the same month, the bigger each bonus gets:

New clients closed this monthBonus for that clientYour running total
1st clientRM100RM100
2nd clientRM150RM250
3rd clientRM200RM450
4th client and beyondRM200 eachRM650+

The streak resets at the start of each calendar month — so there's always a reason to keep going.

Layer 3 — Add-on kicker

+RM100 extra whenever the deal includes an add-on

Every time a client signs up with an add-on (SEO, KOC AI, or a second service), you get an extra RM100 one-time bonus — on top of Layers 1 & 2, and on top of the bigger recurring % the larger fee already gives you. Selling the full package always pays you more.

Layer 4 — Quarterly bonus

+RM300 for a strong, consistent quarter

Close 6 or more new clients in a quarter (about 2 a month) and keep your client retention at 80% or higher, and you earn a RM300 quarterly bonus. This rewards bringing clients who genuinely stick around.

Optional ramp-up sweetener (first 1–3 months): While you're building momentum, you can also earn RM20 for each genuinely qualified appointment that shows up (capped at 8/month, i.e. up to RM160), so you're rewarded for activity even before the deals close. We'll agree together whether to switch this on.

5. The Ground Rules (Fair & Clear for Both Sides)

  • Paid on real payments only. Commissions and bonuses are paid after the client's payment for that month is actually received by J & Y.
  • 30-day settling period. If a brand-new client cancels or refunds within 30 days, the RM100 close bonus for that client is reversed (deducted from your next payout).
  • Who counts as "your" client. A "new client" is a first-time paying client you sourced. Log the lead in our shared tracker before the appointment. Attribution window is 60 days from first contact to closing. Existing/returning clients, leads already in our pipeline, and your own/related accounts don't qualify.
  • If you move on. If the partnership ends, your recurring income on clients you've already signed continues for 3 more months as a thank-you, then stops. (This term is open to discussion before signing.)
  • When you get paid. Monthly, by the 7th of the following month, by bank transfer, against a simple invoice or signed tracker line.
  • Your status. You work as an independent contractor and handle your own taxes. Either side may end the arrangement with 30 days' written notice.
