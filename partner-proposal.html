Sales Partner Program & Commission Agreement

Prepared by JY Marketing Agency, Georgetown, Penang  |  A partnership to grow together

1. The Partnership at a Glance

We're inviting you to become a Sales Partner for JY Marketing Agency. Your role is simple and plays to your strengths:

  • Find potential clients in your own way — your network, social media, outreach, referrals.
  • Qualify them — make sure they genuinely need our marketing / KOC / SEO services.
  • Book the appointment — set up the meeting and hand it over.
  • We close. Joey handles the pitch, the contract, and the delivery.

You focus on what you do best — opening doors. We focus on closing and delivering great work. And here's the best part: you keep earning every single month for as long as that client stays with us. Bring good clients who stay, and you build a growing monthly income.

2. How You Earn

Your rewards come in four layers — designed so you're paid instantly when you close, and continuously every month after.

Layer 1 — Your monthly income

15% of the client's monthly fee — every month they stay

For as long as the client is active and paying, you receive 15% of their total monthly fee. This never stops while they stay — it's your recurring, passive income.

Because it's a percentage of the total fee, the bigger the deal, the more you earn. A client on the RM1,500 basic management package pays you RM225/month. The same client who also takes SEO (RM400) pays a RM1,900 total — so you earn RM285/month instead. Add-ons reward you automatically, forever.

Renewal reward: when a client renews for another contract term, your rate on that client rises from 15% to 20% per month — so a renewed RM1,500 client pays you RM300/month (RM380 with SEO). The longer you keep clients happy, the more you earn.

Layer 2 — Instant close bonus

A one-time cash bonus every time you close a new client

On top of your recurring income, you earn an upfront bonus for each newly signed, paid client. The more you close in the same month, the bigger each bonus gets:

New clients closed this monthBonus for that clientYour running total
1st clientRM150RM150
2nd clientRM250RM400
3rd client and beyondRM350 eachRM750+

The streak resets at the start of each calendar month — so there's always a reason to keep going.

Layer 3 — Add-on kicker

+RM50 extra whenever the deal includes an add-on

Every time a client signs up with an add-on (e.g. SEO at RM400/month, KOC AI, or a second service), you get an extra RM50 one-time bonus — on top of Layers 1 & 2, and on top of the bigger recurring % the larger fee already gives you. Selling the full package always pays you more.

Layer 4 — Quarterly bonus

+RM500 for a strong quarter

Close 6 or more new clients in a quarter (about 2 a month) and you earn a RM500 bonus. A "quarter" is a 3-month block (Jan–Mar, Apr–Jun, Jul–Sep, Oct–Dec), and we simply count the new clients you closed during those months — easy and transparent.

Optional ramp-up sweetener (first 1–3 months): While you're building momentum, you can also earn RM20 for each genuinely qualified appointment that shows up (capped at 8/month, i.e. up to RM160), so you're rewarded for activity even before the deals close. We'll agree together whether to switch this on.

4. Special Add-On — Creative Production Retainer

Separate from your sales role, you've also agreed to help with content & video editing and photo / graphic creation for JY Marketing Agency. This is paid independently of your sales commission — think of it as a second, fixed income on top of everything above.

Creative Retainer

RM800 per month, flat

A simple, predictable RM800/month to handle JY Marketing Agency's ongoing content & video editing and photo / graphic creation. It's a flat monthly amount — not counted per video or per photo.

Editing & creation only — no shooting. Raw footage, photos and briefs are supplied by JY Marketing Agency; you produce the finished, ready-to-post pieces.

  • Paid on the same schedule as your commission — monthly, by the 7th of the following month.
  • Fixed regardless of sales — it doesn't rise or fall with how many clients you close.
  • Fair-workload review: if the monthly volume grows a lot over time, we'll sit down and adjust the amount together.

5. The Ground Rules (Fair & Clear for Both Sides)

  • Paid on real payments only. Commissions and bonuses are paid after the client's payment for that month is actually received by JY Marketing Agency.
  • 30-day settling period. If a brand-new client cancels or refunds within 30 days, the close bonus for that client is reversed (deducted from your next payout).
  • Who counts as "your" client. A "new client" is a first-time paying client you sourced. Log the lead in our shared tracker before the appointment. Attribution window is 60 days from first contact to closing. Existing/returning clients, leads already in our pipeline, and your own/related accounts don't qualify.
  • If you move on. If the partnership ends, your recurring income on clients you've already signed continues for 3 more months as a thank-you, then stops. (This term is open to discussion before signing.)
  • When you get paid. Monthly, by the 7th of the following month, by bank transfer, against a simple invoice or signed tracker line.
  • Your status. You work as an independent contractor and handle your own taxes. Either side may end the arrangement with 30 days' written notice.
